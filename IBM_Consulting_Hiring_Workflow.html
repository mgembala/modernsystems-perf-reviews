IBM Consulting – Hiring Workflow

End-to-end process for Internal Transfers, External New Hires, and Subcontractor Engagements  |  Source: IBM W3 Publisher (IBM Consulting Workforce Management, 2025)

Hiring Track Overview
IBM Consulting uses three distinct hiring tracks depending on the resource type. All delivery hires (Bands 4–9) must follow the Fulfilment Hierarchy — exhausting internal options before external hiring is considered.

Track 1 – Internal Transfer

An IBM employee moves from another Line of Business (LoB), Center of Competency (CoC), or Client Innovation Center (CIC) into IBM Consulting. Delivery Bands 4–9. Follows same approval chain as external hire.

TRAM • MySA / ProM • Avature

Track 2 – External New Hire

Net-new hire from outside IBM into IBM Consulting (Bands 4–9). Requires Open Seat, TRAM approval, external recruitment campaign via Avature, and GEO/FNC/Global Hiring Board approval before offer release.

TRAM • Avature • GEO/FNC Board

Track 3 – Subcontractor / External Consultant

Engaging a Business Partner or non-IBM supplier for project delivery. Used for co-sales, WBS staffing, or when internal skills unavailable. Routed through SPI/Buy@IBM and Ariba for PO creation.

SPI • Buy@IBM • Ariba • TRAM
